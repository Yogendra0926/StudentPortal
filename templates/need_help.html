Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I reset my password?
Open the Change Password option on the login page. Enter your Student ID, current password and new password. If you cannot access your account, contact the website owner.
I can’t log in to my account. What should I do?
Check that your Student ID and password are entered correctly. If the issue continues, contact the website owner using the email or phone number provided on this page.
How can I access my assignments and study materials?
Sign in to YJ-Portal and open the Assignments or LMS section from the navigation menu.
