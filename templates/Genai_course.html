Course Overview

A practical introduction to the field of Generative AI.

Generative Artificial Intelligence focuses on systems that can generate new content such as text, images, code and other forms of data. This course introduces the foundations, tools and real-world applications of generative models and AI-powered solutions.

What You Will Explore

Broad areas and applications related to Generative AI.

Generative Models

Understand how AI systems learn patterns and generate new content from data.

Language Models

Explore language-based AI systems and their ability to understand and generate text.

Prompt Engineering

Learn how instructions and context guide the responses of generative AI tools.

AI Applications

Discover how generative models can be integrated into useful software applications.

Knowledge & Retrieval

Explore approaches for connecting AI systems with external information sources.

Responsible AI

Consider reliability, limitations, privacy and responsible use of generative systems.

Practical Learning Focus

Connect concepts with hands-on experimentation and real-world problem solving.

Build

Experiment with AI tools and develop small generative AI applications.

Apply

Identify practical use cases and design AI-assisted solutions.

Explain

Communicate model capabilities, limitations and design decisions clearly.
